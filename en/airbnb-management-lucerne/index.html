City, lake and mountains

Lucerne brings together city breakers, business guests and holidaymakers heading up the Rigi, Pilatus or Titlis. By the lake, families and couples come for longer stays. Every location needs a different listing and a different pricing strategy.

Holiday apartments by the lake

In Weggis, Vitznau and Gersau guests look for lake views, peace and quiet and a good boat connection. We show exactly that in the listing and explain the journey by boat, train or car.

On site in Lucerne

Lucerne is our home. Our local team handles cleaning, inspections and small repairs, usually the same day in the city.
