FAQ

What owners ask us.

Your question isn't here? Write to us and we'll reply the same working day.

How much will I earn with my apartment?
That depends on location, size and equipment. The calculator above gives a first estimate. After a viewing we give you a range with all assumptions. We do not guarantee income.
What exactly are the 18 or 21 percent charged on?
On nightly revenue after the platform fee. The guest pays the cleaning fee on top, and we charge no commission on it. If the apartment is empty, you pay nothing.
Who pays for cleaning?
The guest. Every booking includes a cleaning fee that the platform pays out to you together with the nightly revenue. In the Complete package we pay the cleaning team and invoice the cleaning to you at the end of the month together with our commission, at cost and without commission. In the Basic package you organise and pay for cleaning yourself.
How long am I committed?
There is no minimum term. You can cancel at any time with 3 months' notice to the end of a month. Confirmed bookings are either completed or handed over in an orderly way.
What about insurance and damage?
Before we start, we check with you whether your building, contents and liability insurance covers short-term letting. We report damage to the platform with photos. In the Complete package we handle small repairs up to the agreed amount, anything above only after your approval.
Does the apartment have to be furnished?
Yes, guests expect a fully equipped apartment. If something is missing, we give you a list and a fixed-price quote. You decide what to buy yourself.
