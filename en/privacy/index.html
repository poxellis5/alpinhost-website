Privacy policy

We take the protection of your data seriously and process personal data in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Controller

R&B Digital Market KlG, brand Alpinhost, Kellerstrasse 29, 6005 Lucerne, Switzerland. Contact: info@alpinhost.ch or WhatsApp as stated in the imprint.

Enquiries

When you send us an enquiry via the form, your message is prepared in WhatsApp and only sent when you tap send yourself. We use your details only to answer your enquiry and do not pass them on.

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising services.

Hosting and fonts

When you visit the website, our hosting provider processes technically necessary data such as your IP address. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which transmits your IP address to Google.

Your rights

You can request information about the data we hold on you at any time and ask for it to be corrected or deleted.
