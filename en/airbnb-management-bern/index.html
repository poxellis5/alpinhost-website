Business guests on weekdays, city breakers at weekends

In Bern, apartments are often booked by business guests during the week and by city breakers at weekends. We adapt prices and minimum stays to this rhythm so there are no gaps.

Clean turnovers, every time

Business guests expect an apartment that works: fast Wi-Fi, a desk, fresh linen. We check this at every turnover with a checklist and photos.

Bern and the area up to Thun

We manage apartments in the city of Bern and the surrounding area as far as Thun and Spiez, the gateway to the Bernese Oberland.
